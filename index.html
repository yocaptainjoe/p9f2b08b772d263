VENNO × Latin Mafia

Latin Mafia

I’ve been trying different sounds on this project, and we’re going for a distinct creative direction for the visuals. A lot of the songs have an upbeat rhythm and vibe, but the lyrics are darker. If I had to describe the songs in one word, it’d be contrast. The album starts in Casa de Campo during the day and ends up in Boca Chica at night.

We’re building a visual identity around those two places. Our animation team is creating a world for the music videos and visualizers, with characters and settings that carry across the album.
